About This Domain

Fishing rods with type, power, and action specifications. Artificial lures with type and buoyancy characteristics. Fishing lines with type, strength, and diameter. Fishing locations with GPS coordinates and visibility settings. Fish species with type classification. Recorded catches with weight, length, location, and gear used. Community posts and discussions. Comments on posts and catches. Registered anglers in the system. Domain entity managed by AnglerNation.
